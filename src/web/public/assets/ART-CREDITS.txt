GPU Survivor — character artwork and photo references

Data-center equipment was generated with OpenAI GPT Image 2 and processed by
scripts/environment-art.py. Floor panels and enemy projectiles are procedural
pixel artwork authored for this project.

Character sheets were generated with OpenAI GPT Image 2 and processed into
palette-limited pixel art. Changes include full-body costume and pose synthesis,
eight directions, animation, background removal, resizing, and portrait cropping.
Jensen and the fictional rivals were generated from descriptions. The remaining
real-person sheets used these photographic likeness references:
